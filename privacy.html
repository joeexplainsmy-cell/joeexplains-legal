Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026 Applies to the joeexplains TikTok posting tool

This tool connects to exactly one TikTok account — @joeexplains — to publish videos to it. It doesn't collect data from anyone else, and it doesn't sell or share anything.

1 What's collected

DataWhy
TikTok access token & refresh tokenLets the tool publish videos to @joeexplains via TikTok's Content Posting API, without needing the password each time.
TikTok Open IDTikTok's stable identifier confirming which account the tokens belong to.
Video files & captionsThe actual content being posted — drafted ahead of time and sent to TikTok when publishing.

No data is collected about people who watch or interact with @joeexplains on TikTok — the tool only acts on the one connected account, it doesn't read audience data.

2 Where it's stored

Tokens and content live in the creator's own private storage and development environment. Nothing is stored on a public server, and nothing is stored longer than needed to keep the posting tool running.

3 Who it's shared with

Only with TikTok itself, as part of calling TikTok's own API to publish content. Nothing is shared with any other third party, advertiser, or data broker.

4 Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Account → Manage account → Apps and websites → Manage. Revoking access immediately stops the tool from being able to post.

5 Changes

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. The effective date above reflects the latest version.

6 Contact

Questions about this policy or how data is handled can be sent to alexcwn@gmail.com.
